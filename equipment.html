Equipment used

  • Cleats- These are used in soccer so you have a strong grip on the grass.
  • Shinguards- These protect your shins and aids in avoiding injury's.
  • Uniforms- Wearing uniforms helps identify each team which will make it easier to tell the teams apart.
  • Ball- You need a ball to play soccer. These come in different sizes but the most used is size 5.
